About

I'm studying CS and Astrophysics at Columbia. I am currently working on building ML tools for improving wafer manufacturing processes on the optics team at AAC Technologies, which specializes in wafer level optics solutions. Previously I have worked on research in Astrophysics, using N-body simulations to understand the formation of stellar streams, and using time series analysis to study magnetic reconnection in coronal holes. I am interested in applying the combination of Physics and CS to produce innovative solutions to physical problems.
